Where the claims come from

Trip details change every semester. If a price or a date on this site and the Campus Recreation site disagree, believe Campus Recreation.

  1. United Nations. “Goal 3: Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages.” sdgs.un.org/goals/goal3.
  2. American College Health Association. American College Health Association–National College Health Assessment III: Georgia Institute of Technology Executive Summary Fall 2022. Silver Spring, MD: American College Health Association, 2022. Wellness Center PDF. Figures used here: 56.6% positive loneliness screen, 37.7% active-adult physical activity guideline, 28.4% highly active guideline, 28.8% any food insecurity, n = 318, response rate 4.0%.
  3. Georgia Institute of Technology. Reducing Health Disparities. Goal 2, Strategy 2E, Cultivate Well-Being Action and Transformation Roadmap, April 2024. PDF. Used for the two-thirds / one-third mental health support finding and the diagnosis gap by gender.
  4. White, M. P., et al. “Spending at Least 120 Minutes a Week in Nature Is Associated with Good Health and Wellbeing.” Scientific Reports 9, 7730 (2019). nature.com/articles/s41598-019-44097-3.
  5. Georgia Tech Office of Sustainability. “Sustainability Next.” sustain.gatech.edu/sustainability-next-plan. Includes President Cabrera’s statement on advancing the full range of the UN goals.
  6. Georgia Tech News Center. “The Sustainability Next Task Force Delivers Vision and Launches Implementation.” October 24, 2022. news.gatech.edu. Lists the six plan priorities.
  7. Georgia Tech Strategic Plan. “Cultivate Well-Being.” strategicplan.gatech.edu/focus/wellbeing.
  8. Georgia Tech Office of Sustainability. “Student Sustainable Living Guide.” sustain.gatech.edu/student-sustainable-living-guide. Names ORGT under physical well-being and two hours outdoors under mental well-being.
  9. Georgia Tech Campus Recreation. “Adventure Trips.” crc.gatech.edu/adventure-trips. Program description, eligibility, fee waiver, registration, group size, and outpost hours.
  10. Georgia Tech Campus Recreation. Sport and rental pages used for trip specifics: backpacking, caving, biking, whitewater kayaking, Wilderness Outpost, CORE, and programs overview.
  11. Georgia Tech Campus Recreation. “Sustainability in the CRC.” Sustainability page. Connects outdoor programming with stress, mood, and contact with nature.
  12. Georgia Tech Engage. “Outdoor Recreation.” Organization profile. Identity statement, sport list, and scale of the volunteer program.
  13. GTORGT. “Experience ORGT.” YouTube. youtube.com/watch?v=GJakSgcYjfM.
  14. Georgia Tech Center for Mental Health Care and Resources. counseling.gatech.edu.
