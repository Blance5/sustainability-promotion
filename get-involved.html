Seedlings

Do this in order

Check that you are eligible. Open-enrollment instructional trips are for current student members because student fees fund them. You must be at least 18, unless you are a current student with a parent or guardian waiver.

Watch the calendar. Registration opens about one month before the trip and is first come, first served at mycrc.gatech.edu. Pay in full when you register. The public schedule lives on the Adventure Trips page.

Ask about the fee waiver if money is the blocker. Pell Grant recipients and students with a Georgia Tech need-based scholarship may be eligible. Start from the adventure trips page or ask at the Wilderness Outpost, 404-894-7420, during open hours. Do not skip a trip quietly because the price looks final.

Attend the pre-trip class. Plan on two to three hours the week of the trip. Paddlers also need the pool swim test. Bring your own food on the trip itself.

Know the cancellation rules before you pay. Account credit steps down as the date gets closer: 100% at 25 or more days out, 50% from 24 to 14 days, and 25% from 13 to 7 days. No-shows are not refunded. If we cancel for weather, you are due a full refund.

The Canopy

If one trip is not the whole story

A single Saturday meets the two-hour target. A habit supports a campus. Open a step to see how participants become the people who run the next trip. That ladder is how ORGT stays student-led inside Campus Recreation.

You learn the sport at the level the listing promises, meet a small group, and see whether time outside helps your week. Then invite one friend who thinks this is not for them.

The Wilderness Outpost rents camping gear, flatwater boats, and bouldering kits, and staff will talk you through a first campsite. Pick-up is generally Thursday and Friday, 2–5 p.m. A rental turns one trip into a practice you control.

Between trips, sport groups train on campus: the climbing wall, pool sessions, and meetings for backpacking, biking, and the other sports.

CORE adds weekly meetings, about one weekend a month, and a student-planned expedition. The published commitment fee has been $200, with financial aid so cost should not stop an application.

Students who do well can be nominated and trained to lead. The program lasts because this year’s participant becomes next year’s instructor. Ask the staff on your trip about orientation.

Bring someone who needs the waiver, the ride, or the invitation

If you have a roommate who studies late, does not have a car, or is quiet about money, send them this page and register for the same beginner trip. A van with an empty seat is a missed chance.
